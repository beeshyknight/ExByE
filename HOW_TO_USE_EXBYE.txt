EXBYE v5.6 – GETTING STARTED

1. EXTRACT THE ZIP and open index.html (or ExByE.html) in Chrome or Edge.
2. SIGN IN. For full access use EXB-CONSULT / 9090.
   Staff users EXB-001 to EXB-030 use codes 4001 to 4030 respectively.
   Example: EXB-014 / 4014. These are local codes, not Firebase passwords.
3. OPEN A PATIENT. Choose Patients (top) or Find a person (left), search for Zoe Carter, Freddy Mason, Tommy Reed, Robby Ellis, Gwen Harris, Isla Bennett or Ethan Okafor, then select the record.
4. CLINICAL NOTES. Choose Record consultation on Patient Home, or Chronology > + New Consultation. Complete and save.
5. REVIEW A JOURNAL ENTRY. Open Chronology, select For review on an entry, choose a recipient staff identifier, enter the reason and due date, then save. Complete the review in the same area later.
6. ASSESSMENTS. Open Gwen Harris > Risk assessments > Comprehensive assessment. Complete domains, Save draft, reopen later, or Sign assessment. The signed assessment adds a journal entry and review recall.
7. CARE PLANS. Choose Care plans to record goals, actions, named professional and review date. Use the plan review functions to record changes.
8. OTHER AREAS. Observations: NEWS2. Tests & results: laboratory panels. Medicines: current medicines and medication entries. Worklists: allocated actions.
9. BACKUP. As EXB-CONSULT choose Tools (top right) > Export backup. Save the JSON file. Restore with Tools > Import backup.
10. SIGN OUT. Use the user identifier control at the top-right. Another staff member can then sign in.

IMPORTANT:
The standalone file saves to the current browser only. It does not share records between devices. Do not use real patient details. The Firebase version needs configuring first; its account passwords are created separately and will not be 4001–4030.

For illustrated instructions open HOW_TO_USE_EXBYE.html.
